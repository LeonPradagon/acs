How can we apply the Japanese concept of 'Ma' (negative space) to modern digital interface design to reduce cognitive load?

10:42 AM
auto_awesome

The concept of Ma is not merely about "emptiness," but the intentionality of the interval. In digital design, we can translate this through three core principles:

Spatial Breath

Moving beyond standard padding to create expansive margins that frame content as a focal point, rather than a commodity.

Temporal Ma

Introducing deliberate micro-delays in interactions to give the user's mind a moment to catch up with the system's state.

By treating white space not as "empty" but as a structural component, we shift the user's experience from processing information to inhabiting the space.
